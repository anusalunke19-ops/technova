❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept? ›
We accept credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and Razorpay Checkout in test mode and live mode.
How long does shipping take? ›
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping is 2–3 days, and overnight shipping delivers the next business day.
Can I track my order? ›
Yes! Use the Order Tracking tool above with your order ID, or check your Account > My Orders page for real-time status updates.
What is your return policy? ›
We offer a 30-day return policy on most items. Items must be in original condition with all accessories. Use the Returns form above to start.
Are my payment details secure? ›
Yes, all payment data is processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant checkout. Card and credentials are never stored on our database.
How do I cancel an order? ›
You can cancel orders with "pending" status from your Account > My Orders page. Contact support for orders already in processing.
